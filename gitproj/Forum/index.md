**The Forum** is a platform designed to produce high-quality, representative opinions on divisive issues. Rather than amplifying the loudest voices or collapsing to false consensus, the Forum mechanically ensures that every significant perspective — every "tribe of thought" — gets a definitive, polished, well-informed statement of its position.

The result is not one opinion, but a structured set of opinions — each representing a real constituency, each refined through deliberation, each accompanied by the reasoning and evidence that supports it.
